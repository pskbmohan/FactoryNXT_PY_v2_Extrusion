What is this?

Maintenance tracks all planned (preventive) and unplanned (breakdown) maintenance work on machines and equipment. Every maintenance job has a type, priority, assigned technician, and status. The system automatically raises preventive maintenance jobs based on machine hours or calendar schedules.

How to use it

  1. Go to Maintenance → All Jobs from the sidebar.
  2. View jobs by status — Waiting, In Progress, Completed, or Overdue.
  3. For a new breakdown, click "New Job" — select machine, describe the issue, set priority.
  4. Assign a technician — they'll see it in their work queue.
  5. For preventive maintenance (PM), the system auto-generates jobs based on schedule — you just assign and close them.
  6. When work is done, the technician clicks "Complete" and logs what was done, parts used, and time spent.

Key fields explained

FieldWhat it means
Job IDUnique maintenance job number (e.g. MNT-2026-088).
MachineWhich machine needs maintenance — links to Machines module.
TypePM (Planned Maintenance — scheduled) or BM (Breakdown Maintenance — unplanned).
PriorityCritical (machine is down), High (urgent), Medium (normal), Low (when convenient).
Assigned ToWhich technician or contractor is responsible for the work.
StatusWaiting (not started), In Progress (work being done), Completed (done), or Overdue (past due date).
Due / CompletedTarget date and actual completion date — used to measure maintenance responsiveness.

💡 Tips

  • Address Critical BM jobs immediately — the machine is down and costing you money every minute.
  • Keep PM schedule tight — skipping preventive maintenance leads to more breakdowns.
  • Track parts used in each job — this helps predict spare parts inventory needs.
