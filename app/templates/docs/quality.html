What is this?

Quality control ensures every product leaving the factory meets spec. When something doesn't — a dimensional error, a surface defect, or a composition issue — a Non-Conformance Report (NCR) is raised. This module tracks every NCR from discovery through investigation to final disposition: Rework, Use-As-Is, or Scrap.

How to use it

  1. Quality inspector goes to Quality → Raise NCR when a defect is found.
  2. Link the NCR to the WO and the specific batch or lot number.
  3. Select severity — Minor (cosmetic), Major (functional), or Critical (safety/compliance).
  4. Fill in defect description — what's wrong, how it was found, photos if helpful.
  5. NCR is automatically assigned to the QA Manager for review and disposition.
  6. QA manager sets Disposition: Rework (fix it), Use-As-Is (acceptable with note), Quarantine (hold), or Scrap (discard).
  7. Once action is complete, the inspector closes the NCR — it's resolved.

Raised ByThe quality inspector who found the defect.
StatusOpen (raised), Investigation (QA reviewing), or Closed (resolved).
DispositionRework (fix it), Use-As-Is (acceptable), Quarantine (hold for review), or Scrap (discard).

💡 Tips

  • Close NCRs quickly — open NCRs block shipments and hold inventory.
  • Use photos in the description — they help the QA manager decide faster.
  • If the same defect appears on multiple WOs, investigate for a systemic issue (die wear, material batch, operator error).
