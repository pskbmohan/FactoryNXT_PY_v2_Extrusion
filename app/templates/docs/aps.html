What is this?

APS is the brain of the scheduling system. Instead of manually dragging WOs onto a calendar, APS automatically sequences all open work orders across all machines to minimise changeover time, respect due dates, and maximise press utilisation — in seconds. It's like having an expert planner working 24/7.

How to use it

  1. Go to APS → Schedule Engine from the sidebar.
  2. Choose a scheduling horizon — e.g. "Next 7 Days" or "Next 14 Days".
  3. Set priorities — choose Due Date First, Minimise Changeover, or Max OEE.
  4. Click "Run Schedule" — the engine computes in under 5 seconds.
  5. Review the Gantt — you can drag bars to manually adjust if needed.
  6. Click "Publish Schedule" to push it to the shop floor. Until published, it's a draft.

Key fields explained

FieldWhat it means
Schedule VersionUnique ID for this schedule run. Each "Run Schedule" creates a new version.
Horizon DaysHow many days ahead the scheduler plans. Longer = more global optimisation.
AlgorithmFIFO (first-come-first-served), Due Date (urgent first), or OEE Optimised.
Schedule Score %How well the schedule meets all constraints. 90%+ is excellent.
Published AtTimestamp when the schedule was pushed live. Blank = still a draft.

💡 Tips

  • Run APS daily — it adapts to new WOs, cancellations, and machine changes.
  • Start with "Due Date First" — it's the safest default for most factories.
  • If the score is below 80%, check for missing dies or material shortages blocking the schedule.
