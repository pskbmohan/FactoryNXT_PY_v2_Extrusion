What is this?

Planning is where you decide WHAT to make and WHEN. You lay out all released Work Orders on a visual calendar, assign them to presses, and confirm the weekly production programme. The planner sees capacity constraints — if Press #1 is already busy on Tuesday, the system warns you before you double-book it.

📸 The weekly planning grid shows WOs slotted across machines and days with capacity utilisation.

How to use it

  1. Go to Planning → Weekly View — the grid shows machines on rows, days on columns.
  2. All Released WOs appear in the "Unscheduled" panel on the right side of the screen.
  3. Drag a WO onto a machine/day cell to schedule it. The system checks for die availability and material stock.
  4. Watch for warnings — red highlights mean double-booking or missing materials.
  5. Save the plan — it becomes the master schedule for the week visible to the shop floor.
  6. Use "Lock Plan" to prevent further changes once confirmed for the week.

Key fields explained

FieldWhat it means
Machine RowEach row is one press or production line.
Day ColumnMonday through Saturday — click a cell to schedule or edit.
WO BlockA coloured bar showing the WO scheduled on that machine/day.
Unscheduled PanelAll Released WOs not yet assigned to a machine/day.
Capacity UtilisationPercentage of available press-hours committed across the week.

💡 Tips

  • For fully automated scheduling, try APS — Advanced Scheduling instead of manual drag-and-drop.
  • Lock the plan on Friday for the following week to give the shop floor certainty.
  • If a WO can't be scheduled (missing die, low stock), the system highlights it — fix the root cause first.
