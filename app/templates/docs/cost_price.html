What is this?

For each part number, Cost & Price Config calculates the total manufacturing cost — material, machine time, labour, energy, and overhead — and suggests a minimum selling price with your target margin. This gives sales accurate pricing without manual spreadsheet calculations.

How to use it

  1. Go to Cost & Price → All Parts from the sidebar.
  2. Select a part number to view or edit its cost structure.
  3. Review the cost breakdown — material, machine time, labour, energy, and overhead.
  4. Adjust cost inputs if needed (e.g. new billet price, updated machine rate).
  5. Set target margin — the system calculates the minimum selling price automatically.
  6. Save the cost config — sales can now quote accurately using these numbers.

Key fields explained

FieldWhat it means
Raw MaterialWeight × billet price per kg — the largest cost component.
Machine CostCycle time × machine hourly rate — includes depreciation and maintenance.
LabourTotal labour hours × hourly rate — operators, inspectors, handlers.
EnergykWh consumed × electricity rate — furnaces and presses are energy-intensive.
OverheadFactory overhead % (rent, admin, utilities) — typically 10–15% of direct costs.
Total CostSum of all components — the break-even cost before profit.
Target MarginDesired profit margin % — set by sales or management.
Break-Even PriceTotal cost + margin — the minimum selling price to hit your target.

💡 Tips

  • Update billet prices monthly — aluminium prices fluctuate and affect margins significantly.
  • If margin is too low, look for process improvements to reduce cycle time or material waste.
  • Use the cost breakdown in sales negotiations — show the customer exactly what they're paying for.
