What is this?

Inventory gives you a real-time view of every material in the factory — raw billets in the store, work-in-progress profiles on trays, finished goods in the warehouse, and coating powders. Stock levels update automatically as material is consumed (by WOs) or received (by Material Receipt).

How to use it

  1. Go to Inventory from the sidebar — you'll see all materials grouped by type (Billets, WIP, Finished, Powders).
  2. Use filters to search by SKU, location, or stock status.
  3. Click any SKU to see the full movement history — receipts, issues to WOs, adjustments.
  4. Watch for Low Stock badges — these trigger alerts and may block production if not replenished.
  5. Export to CSV for stock valuation or reorder planning.

Key fields explained

FieldWhat it means
SKUStock Keeping Unit — unique ID for each material (billet alloy, WIP profile, finished part number).
DescriptionWhat the material is — alloy grade, profile shape, dimensions.
Qty On HandCurrent quantity available — updates in real time as material moves.
UOMUnit of Measure — kg for billets, pcs for profiles, kg for powders.
LocationWhere the material physically is — Billet Store, Aging Area, Warehouse, Coating Store.
Last MovementDate of the last receipt or issue — helps identify stale stock.
StatusIn Stock (green), Low Stock (orange), or Out of Stock (red).

💡 Tips

  • Set minimum stock levels per material — the system will alert you when stock drops below the threshold.
  • Review Low Stock items daily — a missing billet or powder can halt production.
  • Use the movement history to identify discrepancies — if the system says 1,000 but you count 950, investigate.
