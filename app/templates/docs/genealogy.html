What is this?

Genealogy is the visual, interactive version of Traceability. You enter a WO number or lot number and see a clickable family tree of every input and output — raw material in, finished profiles out, which ones passed QC and which became scrap or rework. It's especially useful for root-cause analysis during customer complaints.

How to use it

  1. Go to Genealogy from the sidebar.
  2. Enter a WO number, lot number, or shipment ID in the search box.
  3. The genealogy tree renders — inputs on the left, the WO in the centre, outputs on the right.
  4. Click any node to drill into that record — billet composition, die history, QC results, shipment details.
  5. Use for root-cause analysis: if a customer reports a defect, enter the shipment ID to see which WO produced it, which billets were used, and what the QC results showed.

Key fields explained

FieldWhat it means
InputsBillet lot (material) and Die (tooling) used for this WO.
WO NodeCentral node showing the work order, part number, target quantity, and good/scrap/rework counts.
OutputsThree categories: Passed QC (shipped), Rework (needs fixing), Scrap (discarded).
Lots / ShipmentsWhere the good pieces ended up — links to specific customers.
NCR ReferencesAny non-conformance reports raised against this WO — click to see the defect details.

💡 Tips

  • Use genealogy when a customer calls with a quality complaint — you can trace the issue back to the root cause in minutes.
  • Look for patterns — if multiple WOs from the same billet lot have issues, the material is the problem, not the process.
  • Export the genealogy report for customer audits — it shows your full quality control process.
