What is this?

KPI Alerts automatically watches key factory metrics and sends you a notification the moment something goes outside the normal range. Instead of checking dashboards constantly, the system comes to you — via on-screen alerts, email, or WhatsApp — when OEE drops below 80%, die press count exceeds its limit, or a shipment is overdue.

How to use it

  1. Go to KPI Alerts → Alert Rules from the sidebar.
  2. Click "New Rule" — select the metric you want to watch (OEE, Die Life, NCR Count, Shipment Status, etc.)
  3. Set threshold value and comparison — e.g. "OEE below 80%" or "Die press count above 95% of limit".
  4. Choose notification channel: In-App (bell icon), Email, or WhatsApp.
  5. Set who gets notified — by role (Plant Manager) or specific user (r.kumar@factory.com).
  6. Save the rule — the alert fires automatically whenever the condition is met.

Key fields explained

FieldWhat it means
MetricWhat you're watching — OEE, Die Life %, NCR Count, Shipment Due Date, Billet Stock, etc.
ThresholdThe value that triggers the alert (e.g. 80% for OEE, 95% for die life).
ComparisonHow to compare — below, above, or equals the threshold.
ChannelWhere the notification is sent — In-App, Email, or WhatsApp.
RecipientWho gets notified — a role (all Planners) or a specific person.
SeverityCritical (red), Warning (yellow), or Info (green) — determines urgency.

💡 Tips

  • Start with 5–10 critical alerts — too many notifications cause "alert fatigue" and people ignore them.
  • Use WhatsApp for urgent alerts (OEE drops, critical NCRs) — people see it immediately on their phone.
  • Review alert rules monthly — disable rules that fire too often without action being taken.
