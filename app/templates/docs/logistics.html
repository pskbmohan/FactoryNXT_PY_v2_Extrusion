What is this?

Logistics manages all outbound shipments — packing lists, vehicle loading, weight verification, and dispatch confirmation. A shipment groups one or more WO lots together for a specific customer, generates a delivery note, and records the actual weights to detect short-shipments.

How to use it

  1. Go to Logistics → New Shipment from the sidebar.
  2. Select customer and carrier — auto-fills the delivery address and contact details.
  3. Add WO lots to the shipment by scanning barcodes or selecting from the list.
  4. System calculates theoretical weight from the BOM (piece weight × quantity).
  5. Weigh the loaded vehicle on the weighbridge — enter actual weight.
  6. System checks: actual within ±2% of theoretical → OK. Outside ±2% → investigate (missing pieces? extra material?).
  7. Generate Delivery Note PDF and confirm dispatch — the shipment status updates to "Dispatched".

Key fields explained

Theoretical WeightCalculated from BOM — what the shipment should weigh based on piece count.
Actual WeightWeighed on the weighbridge — entered manually after loading.
Weight CheckOK (within ±2%), Under (-2% or more), or Over (+2% or more) — flags discrepancies.
StatusDraft (building), Approved (ready), Dispatched (gone), or Cancelled.

💡 Tips

  • Always weigh before dispatch — weight discrepancies are the #1 cause of customer complaints.
  • If actual weight is under theoretical, you may have short-shipped — add more pieces or adjust the invoice.
  • Generate the delivery note before dispatch — the driver needs the signed copy.
