What is this?

Every truck delivery of aluminium billets is recorded here. When a supplier delivers, the goods-in team opens Material Receipt, logs the truck, weighs the billets, records the alloy lot number, and enters the chemical composition certificate from the supplier. The system validates the composition against EN 573-3 limits and flags any out-of-spec material before it enters the production flow.

📸 The Material Receipt form — log delivery details and validate composition against EN 573-3.

How to use it

  1. Go to Material Receipt → New Receipt when a truck arrives.
  2. Enter truck reference and supplier name — this is for traceability.
  3. Select Alloy — the EN 573-3 limits auto-populate for that alloy grade.
  4. Weigh the billets on the weighbridge and enter total KG received.
  5. Enter each element from the mill test certificate (Si, Fe, Cu, Mn, Mg, Cr, Zn, Ti).
  6. The system auto-checks: green PASS or red FAIL per element against EN 573-3 limits.
  7. For FAIL: material is quarantined — a QA alert is raised and the lot cannot be used.
  8. For PASS: billets go to Billet Store and are available for WOs immediately.

💡 Tips

  • Never skip the composition check — out-of-spec alloy can ruin an entire production run.
  • Keep mill test certificates on file — customers may request them for traceability audits.
  • If a delivery fails, quarantine immediately — don't let it mix with approved stock.
