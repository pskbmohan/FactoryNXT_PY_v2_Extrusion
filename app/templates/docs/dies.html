Die Management

Track every die in your inventory — its location, life remaining, and repair history.

🏠 Home › Tooling & Equipment › Die Management

What is this?

Dies are the steel tools that give aluminium its shape during extrusion. Every die is expensive (₹50,000–₹5,00,000) and has a limited life measured in press cycles. Die Management tracks every die in your inventory — where it is right now, how many cycles it has left, when it was last nitrided, and whether it's available or in repair.

How to use it

  1. Go to Dies → All Dies to see your complete die inventory as cards.
  2. Use the search bar to find a die by code or profile description.
  3. Click a die to see its full history: all runs, repairs, nitriding events.
  4. When a die is needed for a WO, click "Assign to WO" — it reserves the die for that job.
  5. After a run, the press count updates automatically from the production log.
  6. When press count exceeds the limit, the die is auto-flagged for nitriding — a repair job is created automatically.

Key fields explained

FieldWhat it means
Die CodeUnique ID etched on the die (e.g. DIE-6063-FH-001). Permanent.
Die TypeSolid, Hollow, or Semi-hollow — determines the extrusion method.
Press CountHow many times this die has been used in a press. Increments with every run.
Press Count LimitMaximum cycles before nitriding is required. Set per die type (e.g. 5,000).
Nitriding CountHow many times this die has been re-hardened. Each nitrogen cycle extends life.
Current LocationWhere the die physically is: Die Store, Press #X, Tool Shop, Furnace.
StatusAvailable, In Furnace, In Press, In Repair, or Retired.

💡 Tips

  • Set up a nitriding threshold (e.g. 80% of limit) in Admin → Settings so you get an alert before the die wears out.
  • A die that's been nitrided more than 6–7 times may be nearing end-of-life — plan replacement early.
  • Always return dies to the Die Store after use — "lost" dies cost ₹50,000+ each.
