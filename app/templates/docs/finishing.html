Finishing & Stretching

Straighten extruded profiles and saw them to final customer lengths.

🏠 Home › Tooling & Equipment › Finishing & Stretching

What is this?

After aging, extruded profiles are straightened on a stretcher (to remove the slight bow from quenching), then sawn to final customer lengths. This module tracks each finishing operation: the stretch amount, saw cut lengths, and the final piece count after sawing.

📸 The Finishing Job form — record stretch parameters, cut lengths, and final piece count.

How to use it

  1. Go to Finishing → Active Jobs from the sidebar.
  2. Select a WO to record finishing for — it loads the pre-stretch length from the extrusion log.
  3. Enter the post-stretch length after straightening — the system calculates elongation %.
  4. Enter the target cut length (from the BOM or customer spec).
  5. Enter the number of pieces after sawing — this updates the WO progress.
  6. Enter scrap ends weight — the offcuts that can't be used.
  7. Click "Complete Finishing" — the WO moves to the next step (usually Inspection or Packaging).

Key fields explained

FieldWhat it means
Pre-Stretch LengthLength before straightening — typically the extrusion table length.
Post-Stretch LengthLength after stretching — should be slightly longer due to elongation.
Elongation %How much the profile stretched — calculated from pre/post values. Should be 0.5–1.0%.
Target Cut LengthThe final customer-specified length (e.g. 6,000 mm).
Number of PiecesFinal count after sawing — this is what goes to packaging/shipment.
Scrap EndsWeight of offcuts and end pieces that are too short to use.

💡 Tips

  • Elongation outside 0.5–1.0% may indicate a material or stretching issue — investigate before proceeding.
  • Keep scrap ends low — they represent lost material and revenue.
  • The piece count here becomes the final quantity for shipment and invoicing.
