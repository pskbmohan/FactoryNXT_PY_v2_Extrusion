What is this?

Process Line shows every active WO as it flows through the factory's production steps. You can see — at a glance — which WOs are at Extrusion, which are in the Aging oven, which are at Coating, and which are waiting for Quality inspection. It's your live production pipeline.

📸 The Process Line Kanban board — each column is a process step, each card is a WO.

How to use it

  1. Go to Process Line from the sidebar — the Kanban board loads with all active WOs.
  2. Filter by Machine, Shift, or Date to focus on what matters to you.
  3. Click any WO card to see its full process history, notes, and current status.
  4. Drag a card to move it forward if the system hasn't auto-moved it (e.g. manual transfer).
  5. Watch colours — red cards = overdue at that step; green = on time; orange = approaching deadline.

Key fields explained

FieldWhat it means
WO CardShows WO number, part number, and time spent at current step.
Column (Step)Which process step the WO is currently at: Extrusion, Aging, Coating, etc.
On Time / At Risk / OverdueBadge showing whether the WO is on schedule, approaching the deadline, or late.
Filter: MachineShow only WOs running on a specific press.
Filter: DateShow WOs active on a specific date or date range.

💡 Tips

  • Red cards are your priority — check them first each morning.
  • If a card is stuck in one column too long, it usually means a bottleneck or missing resource.
  • The board auto-refreshes every 30 seconds when operators log output.
