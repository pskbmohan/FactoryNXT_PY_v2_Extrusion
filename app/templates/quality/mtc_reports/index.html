Material Test Certificate (MTC) / Mill Test Report (MTR) Reference
About MTC/MTR Documents:

Material Test Certificates (also known as Mill Test Reports or MTRs) are standardized documents that provide certified test results for materials. They serve as proof of material quality and compliance with specifications.

MTC Contents Include:
  • Certificate Information: Unique certificate number, order references, generation date
  • Material Identification: Alloy designation, heat numbers, batch numbers for full traceability
  • Chemical Composition: Verified elemental analysis confirming alloy specifications
  • Mechanical Properties: Test results including hardness (Webster/Barcol/Vickers), tensile strength, and other relevant properties
  • Quality Verification: Pass/fail status against acceptance criteria
Standards Compliance:

MTCs generated by this system comply with common industry standards for aluminum extrusion materials. Custom templates can be created to meet specific customer or regulatory requirements.
