X-bar and R Chart Reference
X-bar Chart (Process Mean Tracking):
  • Center Line: Grand mean of all subgroup means (X-double-bar)
  • UCL/LCL: ±3 sigma control limits calculated as X-double-bar ± A2 × R-bar
  • Purpose: Detect shifts in process average over time
R Chart (Process Variation Tracking):
  • Center Line: Average of all subgroup ranges (R-bar)
  • UCL/LCL: Control limits calculated as D4 × R-bar and D3 × R-bar
  • Purpose: Detect changes in process variability/spread
